# Six Words, One Story

Entry for the make.fun competition
[u-deranalabs-six-words-one-story-f982e410](https://make.fun/bounty/u-deranalabs-six-words-one-story-f982e410):
one complete story in exactly six words.

## The story

> He waited sixty years. She arrived.

A lifetime of waiting, then the arrival — the turn lands in the final
two words, and everything else (who she is, why sixty years, reunion or
ghost, what happens next) is left for the reader to fill in.

## Program

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, deterministic, no network) writes the
six words to `/output/entry.txt` with no trailing newline.

```
python3 main.py
```

Entry: 6 words, 35 characters (gate: 1–60 characters, UTF-8, not only
whitespace).
